# Vision

Status: draft from the owner's kickoff conversation (2026-11-24); the owner refines it in the
first refinement session.

## The game in one paragraph

In the middle of winter, a mother and her two children survive a cart crash in a snowy wood and
wake up with no memory of their past; they find their way to a ruined old manor. The player helps
the family survive the winter and slowly turn the manor into a home.
At every step the player chooses how to help, and the choices change what happens: who the family
trusts, what they keep, what they lose, and how the story ends. Some choices lead into short
minigames (keep the fire alive, find firewood, fix the roof, cook from what is left); how well the
player does changes the story, but never stops it.

## Why it exists

Mobile ads for this genre show exactly this story and these choices, and then the actual game is
match-3 or merge with pay pressure. Don't Give Up is the game those ads promise: the story and the
choices are the product.

## Pillars

1. **The story is the game.** Characters people care about, a world that remembers what you did.
2. **Choices matter.** Every choice is a real trade-off and changes state the story reads later.
3. **Minigames are fun on their own.** Short, tactile, made for touch first; they change the
   outcome by degree.
4. **Honest to the player.** See the honesty rules below.
5. **Warm look.** A colourful take on 1930s rubber-hose cartoons (docs/art-style.md).

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

- No match-3, no fake-ad gameplay, no misleading store pages or marketing.
- No energy timers, no waiting to play, no paywalled story progress, no loot boxes.
- No fake urgency, guilt prompts or "the children will freeze unless you pay".
- A minigame never blocks the story: a poor result leads to a different story, not a wall.
- Monetization, if any, is decided by the owner and stays fair (for example a premium price or a
  one-time unlock after a free first chapter). Agents never add monetization on their own.

## Platforms

Web first (desktop and phone browsers), then iOS and Android through Capacitor.

## Languages

English and Hungarian from the start; more later.

## Audience

Players who like story games and the "help her rebuild" fantasy but are tired of being tricked.
Age rating target: 12+ (themes of hardship, no graphic content).

## Out of scope (for now)

Multiplayer, accounts, online features, user-generated content.
